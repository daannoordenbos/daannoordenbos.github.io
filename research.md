My research focuses on optimal stopping with limited information, prophet inequalities, and random walks.

## Publications

### Bayesian Optimal Stopping with Maximum Value Knowledge

Pieter Kleer and Daan Noordenbos  
*ACM Transactions on Economics and Computation*, 2026

We study an optimal stopping problem with \\(n\\) correlated offers when only the distribution of their maximum is known, rather than the full joint distribution. We determine the optimal deterministic policy and provide an upper bound on the performance of the optimal randomised policy. Under additional smoothness assumptions, we establish a tight convergence rate as \\(n\\) increases.

[Paper](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=nl&user=4yTbvv0AAAAJ&citation_for_view=4yTbvv0AAAAJ:u5HHmVD_uO8C)

## Theses

### Master's Thesis — Optimal Strategies for Olympic Weightlifting

Groningen, 2024

In this thesis competitive Olympic weightlifting is modelled as a Markov decision process and game-theoretic methods are used to determine strategies that maximise the probability of winning. The relevant subgames are identified, the uniqueness of their perfect equilibrium is established, and an efficient algorithm for computing it is provided.

[Thesis](https://daannoordenbos.github.io/master-thesis/)

### Bachelor's Thesis — Efficiency of Hamiltonian Monte Carlo for Hierarchical Bayesian Mixed Logit Estimation

Groningen, 2023

This thesis compares Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC) with random walk Metropolis-Hastings (RWMH) for hierarchical Bayesian mixed logit estimation. The results show that HMC provides substantially better estimation speed and convergence, although its advantage diminishes as the number of dimensions increases.

[Thesis](https://daannoordenbos.github.io/bachelor-thesis/)
